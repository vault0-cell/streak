THE RULES

Fixed before launch. The engine runs them exactly as written — no discretion, no exceptions.

Survivor = $20+ and zero movement for 48h

Your balance is read on-chain each round. Sells and transfers both count as movement. Split your bag across wallets and each wallet must qualify on its own.

Square-root weighting

Shares follow √(balance). Whales earn more, but never dominate — small survivors get a real slice of every round.

Jackpot every 144 rounds — or at 1 SOL

Roughly once a day, one survivor wins the entire pool, drawn with weight √(bag) × streak. The draw uses the finalized blockhash of the round, so anyone can recompute the winner from the published report.

Dust is never lost

Shares below 0.0001 SOL are carried forward until they clear the threshold, then paid in batches of up to 18 wallets per transaction. Every payment has a public transaction link.

FAQ

Do I need to connect my wallet or claim anything?

No. If your wallet qualifies at a round, SOL is sent to it. There is nothing to sign and no site to trust with your keys.

Where does the SOL come from?

Trading fees. Every $STREAK trade on pump.fun pays a creator fee; fee sharing routes 100% of it to the engine. A round pays out only what trading actually brought in — nothing is promised up front, and quiet rounds pay little or nothing.

What happens if I sell a little?

Any outbound movement resets your streak to 0 and you sit out 48 hours. SOL you already earned stays yours.

Can the rules or the split change?

The fee-sharing setup is locked at launch. If the engine parameters ever change, the change is announced in advance and every past round stays published.

Is this affiliated with pump.fun?

No. STREAK is an independent token that trades on pump.fun; the engine is run by the STREAK team.
