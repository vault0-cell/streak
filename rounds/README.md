# Round reports

After launch, **every round of the Survivor Engine is published here** —
one JSON report and one human-readable Markdown report per round:

```
rounds/round-0001.json
rounds/round-0001.md
rounds/round-0002.json
rounds/round-0002.md
...
```

## What a report contains

- The pot: creator fees claimed for that round, and the exact 50 / 30 / 20
  split in lamports (the three slices always sum to the pot).
- The holder snapshot: every wallet's balance, its USD value, and the
  snapshot hash.
- The survivor list with streaks and √balance weights — and the
  non-survivors, with the reason each one missed (sold in window, below the
  $20 minimum, …).
- The buyback: quote, tokens bought, burn transaction.
- The payout plan and, once sent, every payout transaction signature
  (batches of ≤18 wallets).
- The jackpot: pool before/after, and on draw rounds the slot, the
  finalized blockhash, all draw weights and the winner — everything needed
  to recompute the draw independently (see "How to verify" in the root
  README).

## Publishing routine

Reports are generated by the engine at the end of each round and committed
to this directory as rounds complete. Reports are append-only: a published
report is never edited. If a report is ever found to be wrong, the
correction is published as a new file and announced — the original stays.

State that carries between rounds (streaks, dust balances, jackpot pool,
lifetime totals) is tracked by the engine and summarised in each report's
`totals` block, so the whole history can be replayed from round 1.

## Samples (pre-launch)

`sample/` contains three rounds produced by the engine's public simulation
against a synthetic 30-wallet fixture (22 survivors, a forced jackpot draw
in round 3). They are format examples, **not** real payouts — the fixture
mint, wallets and blockhash are synthetic. Real reports replace them here
from launch day.
